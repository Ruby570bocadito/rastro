## Caveats

- Each brand mixes every group that ever imitated it, often across years and time zones, so a rhythm here is a lower bound on what a single campaign shows.
- crt.sh answers broad wildcard searches with a partial sample, not the full history.
- Wildcard answers carry no CT log time. Times come from Let's Encrypt notBefore plus its one-hour backdate (checked on two certificates, within two minutes). Certificates from other CAs are left out of the timing.
- Hosting panels may issue a certificate minutes after the person adds the domain, and on their own schedule.
- The working-day profile is a typical one; groups on night shifts shift the estimate.
