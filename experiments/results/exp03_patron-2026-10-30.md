Run on 2026-10-30. 161,345 registrable domains from Phishing.Database. 72 families (same brand and token layout, at least 15 domains); the 40 largest were tested. Each pattern was learned from 10 domains of its family.

- Median recall on the rest of the family: **53%**
- Families where the pattern catches at least 80 %: 4 of 40
- Domains outside the family that some pattern matches: 218
- Narrow patterns (at most 10^9 possible names, the kind worth watching live): 5 of 40, median recall **87%**, spill-over 0

## Reading it

- High recall with a broad pattern ([a-z]{m,n} slots) catches siblings but risks false alarms on benign names.
- Narrow patterns (a vocabulary of words and a few TLDs) fire rarely, so they are the ones worth watching live.
- Families here are grouped by brand and layout, not by confirmed campaign, so some mix several crews.
